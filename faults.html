Fault 03

Forearm Flyout

From the coach’s Forearm Flyout notes

What it looks like

Stay connected, with the right tension and alignment. Disconnection is something you recognize by feel. The arm unravels too soon when it does not engage early.

Watch the takeaway. A neutral, supinated, or pronated takeaway changes the tension.

Why it matters

The notes tie disconnection to pain, tightness, or injury in the elbow, bicep, forearm, or anterior shoulder. Consistency and command drop, especially on breaking balls. Recovery is slower. Long toss stalls under 250 ft.

A long arm can feel like leverage and still lose efficiency and velocity.

Corrections

  • Pay attention to takeaway posture.
  • Keep posture and glove-side discipline.
  • Use up-tempo drills to cut excess tension.

Training tools named in the notes: SuperPro, bell clubs, wrist weights, and Connection Balls (#1 position).

Daily routine

These volumes are the corrective routine from the notes. Do not add them on top of a stage day unless your coach builds them into the plan.

  1. Decel (Marshall 1’s and Marshall 2’s) — Decel category, Stage II, Marshall 1’s, Marshall 2’s
  2. Walking Torques (10 min) — original card, Stage II
  3. Quick Picks (15–20 throws) — Stage II
  4. Jump Backs — Stage II
  5. Double Plays without Connection Ball — Stage II
  6. Double Plays with Connection Ball — same Double Plays card
  7. Regular pitching without Connection Ball — the notes say regular pitching. On this site that is Stretch and Full. Pro examples are on those cards.
