Fault 03

Forearm Flyout

What it looks like

Stay connected, with the right tension and alignment. Disconnection is something you recognize by feel. The arm unravels too soon when it does not engage early.

Watch the takeaway. A neutral, supinated, or pronated takeaway changes the tension.

Why it matters

Disconnection can bring pain, tightness, or injury in the elbow, bicep, forearm, or anterior shoulder. Consistency and command drop, especially on breaking balls. Recovery is slower. Long toss stalls under 250 ft.

A long arm can feel like leverage and still lose efficiency and velocity.

Corrections

  • Pay attention to takeaway posture.
  • Keep posture and glove-side discipline.
  • Use up-tempo drills to cut excess tension.

Training tools: SuperPro, bell clubs, wrist weights, and Connection Balls (#1 position).

Daily routine

Keep this routine separate from an ENHANCE day. Add these throws only if your coach builds them into the plan.

  1. Decel (Marshall 1’s and Marshall 2’s) — Decel category, ENHANCE · Stage 2, Marshall 1’s, Marshall 2’s
  2. Walking Torques (10 min) — RAMP, ENHANCE · Stage 2
  3. Quick Picks (15–20 throws) — ENHANCE · Stage 2
  4. Jump Backs — ENHANCE · Stage 2
  5. Double Plays without Connection Ball — ENHANCE · Stage 2
  6. Double Plays with Connection Ball — same Double Plays card
  7. Regular pitching without Connection Ball — Stretch and Full
