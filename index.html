ORIGINAL COLOR PROGRESSION0 / 7 reviewed

The color progression is the reference chart: colors, sets, and reps. Stages I–V do not use these colors. Ask your coach for the weight of each color. Start at Marshall 1’s.

Before you throw

Listen to your arm.

“Whenever you throw—listen to your arm. You’ll know when you’re stretching yourself too much if you pay attention. Never throw in a way in which you lose control. Control should never leave.”

Stop if you have pain or lose control. Tell your coach or parent. Do not throw through pain.

2× max / weekFull routine at high effort.First 4 drillsMedium to low effort only, with a coach’s plan.

How to use this guide

Quality before distance.

  1. Warm up with your coach. Confirm your ball weights, effort, and total throwing workload.
  2. Start with Marshall 1’s at 30 feet. Follow the sets below; each colored chip is one set.
  3. Use the 6/9 catchable-throw check with your coach. Distance is earned, not rushed.
  4. Watch the full demo, slow it down, then use “Replay key move” to see the action again.

For youth players: weighted-ball work and high-effort throwing require an individualized, supervised plan. This guide is not medical advice or a substitute for your coach’s assessment.

How to score this progression

Start with 8–10 throws at Marshall 1’s, 30 feet. If you use plyos, split the throws across the balls, at least 3 throws per ball. The sets on each card follow that chart. Double Crow Hop is 6–10 throws.

If 6 of 9 throws are catchable with the catcher in his stance, move to the next distance and movement. If not, repeat this movement and distance. After 5 rounds, go to the stretch, then the full delivery.

Ask your coach before you start. “5 rounds” needs a definition, and the 6/9 check has to fit the throws already planned. Do not add five full circuits or nine extra test throws. Your coach sets the rounds and how to score the check.

The first four drills can be medium to low effort when your coach says so. Ball color is not a weight. Ask your coach for the ounces.
